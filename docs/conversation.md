## Phase 2: lesson authoring and adversarial review

Model routing: every lesson was written by a **Sonnet** author agent (medium effort), then checked by a separate **Sonnet** reviewer at high effort that was told to *find problems*. Reviewer issues went back to a Sonnet fixer, then to a fresh reviewer, up to two rounds. A lesson only counts as passed when a reviewer returns no blocker or major issue.
